Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Hi. We're Just Tap Apps LLC, a small, independent app studio based in Florida. This policy explains what information our apps collect, why we collect it, and the control you have over it. It covers Snooze Bully and this website. We'll add sections for Atlas Noches and Travel Shakeup before those apps launch.

The short version

  • We collect only what each app needs to work.
  • We don't sell your personal information. Ever.
  • We don't show ads, and we don't share your data with advertisers.
  • You can delete your account and your data whenever you want.
  • Questions? Email support@justtapapps.com.

What we collect

Your account

Your name and email address when you sign up, and your login details if you sign in with another service.

Subscriptions

Whether you have an active subscription, and which plan. Payments are handled by Google Play. We never see or store your card details.

Support messages

If you email us, we keep the conversation so we can help you.

Crash and diagnostic data

When the app crashes, we receive a technical report (device model, operating system version, app version and what went wrong) so we can fix it. We set these reports up to leave out your name, email and messages.

Snooze Bully

  • Your name for wake-ups: the name you type so personas can greet you.
  • Custom messages: any message you type to be turned into a wake-up.
  • Alarm settings: alarm times, which personas you pick and your Defier mode setting.
  • Wake-up clips: audio and video clips of your wake-up are kept only for a short time and deleted automatically, unless you choose to save or share them.
  • Friends: the friends you connect with, backup alarms they set for you, and clips you send each other.

To create spoken wake-ups, we send text (such as a wake-up line, your name or your custom message) to our AI service providers listed below. We don't send them your email address.

How we use it

  • To run the app: your account, alarms, wake-ups and friend features.
  • To manage your subscription.
  • To answer support requests.
  • To fix bugs, prevent abuse and keep the service secure.

We don't use your information for advertising, and we don't sell it.

Who we share it with

Only service providers that help run our apps, and only what each one needs:

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect our users, our apps or the public.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account is open. Wake-up clips are deleted automatically after a short time unless you save or share them. When you delete your account, we delete your data as described on our Delete Account page.

Security

We protect your information with encryption in transit, restricted access and security controls on our databases and servers. No system is perfectly secure, but we take protecting your data seriously.

Your choices

  • See, correct or delete your information by emailing support@justtapapps.com.
  • Delete your account any time in the app under Settings → Delete my account.
  • Turn off app permissions (such as notifications) in your phone's settings.

Children

Our apps aren't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For important changes, we'll let you know in the app.
