Delete Your Account

Last updated: November 17, 2026

You can delete your account and its data at any time, for Snooze Bully and any other Just Tap Apps app.

Two ways to delete

  1. In the app: open Settings → Delete my account and confirm.
  2. By email: send a message from the email on your account to support@justtapapps.com with the app name and "Delete my account" in the subject. We'll confirm when it's done.

Cancel your subscription first

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

What gets deleted

Your account, name, email, alarm settings, custom messages, voice greetings, saved clips and friend connections. We delete them within 30 days of your request.

What we may keep

Records we're required to keep by law, such as purchase and tax records, and support emails needed to resolve an open issue. Google Play keeps its own records of your purchases.
